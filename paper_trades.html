Caveats
  • Sample: one year of signals (2025-10-07 to 2026-10-06, 251 sessions) - the cache holds ~2 years of bars and the first year is needed as look-back (52-week highs, 200-day MAs, RS). One market path; bands / families with < 30 trades are flagged thin and should not be trusted.
  • Point in time: bars are cut at each session, the universe and RS come from that day's ranking file (feeds/rs/cache/ranks), names delisted since the cache was built are still present (the cache stores every listing per day), so survivorship is limited to tickers whose identity changed (split re-adjustment from the newest splits file; >4x / <0.15x jumps cut history).
  • Not replayable, dropped from the replay score: desk family (Minervini report / watchlist - only 5 dated reports exist), alert states (EXTENDED / FAILED BREAKOUT / weak-volume -8), group strength (+2), second-chance flag (+3). The replayed scores are therefore slightly lower / noisier than the live score; the A+++ desk family can only come from base here.
  • Earnings: the -15 penalty and the A+++ 5-session rule use ESTIMATED report dates (SEC quarter ends + each name's own reporting lag); can be off by days. Qullamaggie EPs skip the SEC catalyst check (A-grade KK EPs accepted); market caps are today's caps scaled by price.
  • Short-history names (IPOs < 126 sessions with provisional RS) are not in the historical ranking files, so they are missing from the replay (the live scan includes them) - the replay under-samples young power-play names.
  • Execution: EOD bars only. Buy stops fill at max(open, stop) when the high crosses; an intraday fill + stop on the same bar is booked as a stop (conservative). Trail exits are close-basis and fill at that close (in practice a next-open exit could be better or worse). Slippage 0.1% per side; no commissions; no position limits except in the $100k portfolio test.
  • Trades still open on the last session are marked to market at that close (counted in the stats, flagged open_at_end).
  • Overlapping trades: unconstrained stats treat every signal as a separate $10k trade, so they are not an account return; the portfolio test ($100k, 10 slots, best score first) is the account-like view.
Trade rules
(shared with backtest_setup_master.py - this module is the ONE implementation)
  Entry   buy stop at Setup Master's buy stop (plan.entry). Fills on a later session when high >= buy stop, at max(open, buy stop)
          (a gap open above fills at the open) + slippage. Unfilled orders expire after EXPIRY_SESSIONS sessions, or are cancelled
          when a session closes below the stop before a fill.
  Size    POSITION_USD notional (default $10,000) -> shares = floor(notional / fill); results also in R (size independent).
  Stop    Setup Master's structure stop (plan.stop). A session with low <= stop exits at min(open, stop) (gap through = the open;
          on the fill session itself at the stop).
  Exits   PRIMARY = ATR trail with Chris's DEEP ZONE (research-tools/psie/exits.py deep_zone_exit, close mode, same params):
          trail line = highest high since entry - 3.0 x Wilder ATR(14); price may close back to the line and up to 1 ATR below
          it; a close below line - 1 ATR exits at that close. The initial stop stays the hard floor.
          Comparison models on the same fill: plain ATR trail (close < line), fixed 2R, fixed 3R, 50% off at 2R + rest deep-zone.
          2R / 3R hits = the high reached entry + 2R / 3R while the primary trade was open.
  Costs   SLIPPAGE_PCT per side (default 0.1%): fills x (1 + s), exits x (1 - s).
