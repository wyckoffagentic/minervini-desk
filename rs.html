ℹ️ How the overall score works

Overall (0–100) = 35% RS + 35% earnings + 15% sector bullishness + 15% peer momentum. RS and earnings carry most of the weight because Minervini’s leaders combine price leadership with earnings power; sector and peer momentum confirm the group is in play (most big winners come from leading groups) without letting a hot group carry a weak stock.

  • RS = 70% × IBD-style RS rating (percentile across the ranked universe) + 30% × RS rank within its SIC industry group (percentile).
  • Earnings = the earnings score; limited-data names get a neutral 45 (tagged) instead of zero.
  • Sector bullishness (SIC industry group with ≥ 8 ranked names, else the sector): share passing the Trend Template (30), share above the 50-day (20), median 1/3/6-month return percentile among groups (25), group median RS percentile (25).
  • Peer momentum: share of group members with RS 90+ (40), members in the RS Top 50 (20), actionable scanner breakouts (15), 4-week change in the share with RS 80+ (25). Leader = top 20% of its group by RS, laggard = bottom half.
