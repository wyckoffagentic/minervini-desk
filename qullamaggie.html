Setups

  • Breakout: a big move (30–100%+) in the past 1–3 months. Then an orderly pullback with higher lows and a tightening range, usually 2 weeks to 2 months, surfing the rising 10/20-day MA. Then a range expansion. [1]
  • Episodic pivot: unexpected news, mostly earnings or guidance, gaps the stock 10%+ on big volume. Best when the stock had not already rallied for 3–6 months. A gap that sells off is not an EP. [1] [2]

His scans (run here)

  • Momentum: 1-day dollar volume over $150M, ADR% (20) over 3.5, ranked on close ÷ lowest low of 22 bars (1M) and 65 bars (3M), top 90% band, tightened to 95% above 50 hits. [3] 6M uses 126 bars (desk assumption).
  • EP: up more than 7.5% on the day, close above yesterday's high, over $100M traded. [4] The ticker check then wants a 10%+ gap and real news. [2]

Entry & stop

  • Buy the opening-range high (first 1-, 5- or 60-minute candle) as the stock breaks out. [1]
  • Stop = low of the day, no wider than 1× ADR on breakouts, at most 1.5× on EPs. [1] [2]
  • On this page, Trigger = the range high (EP: the high since the gap). The plan stop is the tighter of the last session's low and Trigger − 1 ADR (EPs 1.5 ADR). Once live, the stop is the breakout day's low.

Sizing

  • Risk 0.25–1% of the account per trade, rarely more than 1%. [1]
  • Most positions are 10–20% of the account. Never hold more than 30% in one stock overnight. [1]

Selling

  • Sell 1/3–1/2 after 3–5 days and move the stop to break-even. [1]
  • Trail the rest on the 10-day MA (fast stocks, and beginners) or the 20-day. Exit on the first close below it. [1] [8]

Market

  • Index 10-day MA above the 20-day and both rising = the good market. 10 below 20 = caution; both falling = probably no breakouts. [6] [7]
  • In down-trending markets breakouts don't work well. Trade less and smaller, or sit in cash. [8]
  • Aggressive / Selective / Cash above = the rulebook's green / yellow / red (§7, §11.0), on QQQ with IWM as the small-cap check.

Where the desk deviates

  • Price ≥ $5 (he trades lower). Breakout watch uses the desk liquidity floor (ADV $20M, §11.1) because volume dries up in a flag; his $150M is a 1-day scan filter.
  • If a momentum window has fewer than 20 hits at $150M, it drops to his May 2020 floor ($60M), then $20M, and says so.
  • EPs from the last 10 sessions stay on watch. A desk tier (≥$20M on ≥3× volume) shows smaller names on the page only.
