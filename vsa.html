Bar measures: spread = high−low ÷ average of the prior 20 bars (wide ≥ 1.25×, narrow ≤ 0.75×); close position 0 (low) – 1 (high); volume ÷ prior 20-bar average (ultra high ≥ 2×, high ≥ 1.3×, low ≤ 0.8×, ultra low ≤ 0.5×); up/down bar = close vs prior close. Key rules: selling climax = wide down bar to a new 20-bar low on ≥ 2× volume after a decline, closing off the lows (or next bar up); stopping volume = heavy down bar in a decline closing in its upper half; no supply / no demand = narrow down / up bar on volume below the prior two bars; test = dips under the prior low, closes in the top 40% on low volume; upthrust = new short-term high, wide spread, closes on the low; buying climax = wide up bar into new highs on ≥ 2× volume closing mid/low after a run; end of a rising market = narrow up bar on ≥ 1.8× volume at new highs. Sequences: climax/stopping volume → low-volume test (→ wide up bar = demand returning); upthrust → no demand; climax top → no demand/upthrust. Balance 0–100 = recency-weighted sum of signs and sequences plus the up-vs-down-bar volume ratio (≥ 60 strength, ≤ 40 weakness). Thresholds are desk calibration (Williams/Holmes publish none); pseudo-upthrust and squat are marked unverified in the method file. Confirmation uses the next bar, so older signs carry one bar of hindsight; today's bar is "awaiting next bar".
