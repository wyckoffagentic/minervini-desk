How the score works

Every desk scan votes. A name scores 0–100 = confluence (how many independent scan families flag it) + quality + actionability − penalties. Scans in the same family count once (a VCP that is also a SEPA base is one base vote).

PartMaxRule
Confluence35Families: base pattern (VCP/SEPA/flat base/power play/HTF) · Wyckoff spring (range or uptrend; actionable, or confirmed/in test within ~30 days daily, 10 weeks weekly, 4 months monthly) · MA Stack (flip/pullback/GUD/high-RS PB/W→D) · 10/20 EMA pullback · new 52w/ATH (attention) · Minervini desk (report or watchlist). 1 family = 12, 2 = 24, 3 = 32, 4+ = 35.
Quality35RS ≥95 +10, ≥90 +8, ≥80 +5, ≥70 +2 · trend template +6 · tightness (last-10-day range ≤6% +4, ≤10% +2; vol10/50 ≤0.8 +3, ≤1.0 +1; cap 6) · VSA balance ≥70 +5, ≥60 +3, +2 for a no-supply/test in the last 2 weeks (cap 6; VSA is context only) · spring confirmed/SOS +4, in test +2 · RS line new high in the last week +3 · strong group (bull score ≥70) +2 · RS top-50 +1.
Actionability30Uses the Minervini desk's pivot/stop when the desk has one, else the most actionable flagged entry ( SEPA, VCP, flat base/power play, GUD plan, uptrend spring, range spring, EMA zone). Price vs entry: 0 to −3% (at the pivot) 18 · 0 to +3% (fresh trigger) 16 · −3 to −6% 10 · +3 to +5% 8 · −6 to −10% 4. Risk to stop: ≤5% 12 · ≤8% 9 · ≤10% 4 · ≤12% 1.
Penalties—Earnings within ~7 sessions −15 (dates from yfinance, unverified) · breakout on weak volume −8 · below the 50 EMA with the stack broken −10.
Excluded—Failed breakout (alert / SEPA buy failed / failed spring) · extended (alert EXTENDED or >5% past entry) · risk >12% · liquidity: price <$5 or 50-day average dollar volume <$20M (research-tools/liquidity.py; listed under “Hidden for liquidity”) · not in the RS universe · no entry/stop in any feed.
