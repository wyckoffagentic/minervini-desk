M BRAIN — a long-only what would Mark Minervini do decision rulebook

Research only, not advice. Built 11 Oct 2026 (Sydney) for Chris's desk.

What this is. A step-by-step, long-only rulebook that tries to take discretion out of live trading. It encodes Mark Minervini's (MM) SEPA method: market regime first (follow-through, distribution, breadth, "traction"), a universe of confirmed stage-2 leaders (Trend Template + RS), setups that show volatility contraction into a pivot (VCP, flat base, cup completion cheat, power play), a buy only at the pivot on volume, a pre-set stop that is never wider than 10%, risk of 1.25–2.5% of equity per trade, progressive exposure, protecting breakeven, and selling into strength as well as into weakness.

What this is not. No short selling of any kind. MM's stage-4 shorts, his SPY/QQQ/IWM hedges, inverse ETFs and every short-related post are left out on purpose (the X filter dropped them, see §15). It is not a market-timing model for index trading, and it is not investment advice.

Backbone and additions. The backbone is the desk's Minervini notes (blog excerpts of his books, X posts, interviews), cited as §n. Additions come from MM's own X posts, pulled 11 Oct 2026 into the desk's X-post archive and cited by URL. A separate fan quote account (@MinerviniQuote) is in §13B.

0.1 Daily operating sequence (pre-commitment)

  1. Before the open: read the regime (§1) from the desk's market feed (distribution count, FTD status, % of stocks above the 50/200-day, new highs vs lows) and from your own last 10 trades. Write the exposure ceiling for the day (R10).
  2. Refresh the universe: Trend Template 8/8 and RS ≥ 70 (U1–U3); drop anything in a stage-4 downtrend (U4).
  3. Build tomorrow's list: only names with a qualifying base and a defined pivot (V1–V14), with the stop and share count already written (S1, Z1–Z3).
  4. In session: buy only as price clears the pivot on projected volume (N1–N3). No pivot, no volume, no buy.
  5. After the close: move stops per M3–M6, check sell rules (P1–P12), log every trade and mistake (O9).

0.2 The do-nothing list — when MM stays out

  • No stocks meet the criteria → cash. "If there are no stocks that meet my criteria, I stay in cash." (R7)
  • Indexes are rallying but few stocks are in buyable position → wait (R8, O3).
  • Distribution soon after a follow-through day → no aggression (R3).
  • Stock fails any Trend Template item (except the documented low-cheat exception) → not a candidate (U1, V12).
  • Stock is more than 5% past its pivot, or the stop would be wider than 8% (10% absolute) → pass (N5, S3).
  • You are on an abnormal losing streak → smaller, not bolder (Z6, O2).
  • Earnings inside the gate and no cushion → no new position (A1).
  • Base deeper than 50% or a late-stage (4th–5th) base → pass (V8, V10).

1. Regime (run first, every day)

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
R1 IF you want to know when to buy → THEN react to how stocks and the tape act; don't predict. "Your job as a trader is NOT to try to anticipate that event, but rather react as the proverbial winds change." [MM-own words] §7.5
R2 IF there is no bona fide follow-through day → THEN treat any rally as unconfirmed. He defines it: "a strong advance of 1% or more on increased volume—accompanied by broad participation and a plentiful number of individual stocks breaking out and following through successfully." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/2102810522273886230 (23 Sep 2026)
R3 IF distribution appears soon after a follow-through day → THEN red flag; no aggression. "When you see distribution soon after a follow-through day, it’s a red flag!" A higher-volume day with a bad close the day after can nullify the FTD: "if today's action is on higher volume with a bad close, the follow through day is nullified." [MM-own words] §7.1; https://x.com/markminervini/status/1526974157933760512 (18 May 2022)
R4 IF distribution dominates and breakouts fail → THEN do not get aggressive. "As long as distribution continues to dominate the general averages and individual stock breakouts fail to follow through and gain traction, the time to get aggressive in stocks has NOT yet arrived." [MM-own words] §7.2
R5 IF the tape is dominated by distribution and few stocks are buyable → THEN treat rallies as counter-trend. "I treat all rallies during a correction as nothing more than counter trend reactions. The market must prove itself to get my hard-earned capital at risk." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1719712431046357276 (1 Nov 2023)
R6 IF the indexes are at highs but breadth is weak (% of stocks above the 50- and 200-day near lows, A/D line making lower lows) → THEN "stealth correction": light exposure, tight stops. "Patience and caution still advised. Exposure should be light with stops tight." [MM-own words] §7.6, §7.7; https://x.com/markminervini/status/2104590683218624704 (29 Sep 2026); https://x.com/markminervini/status/2108201530570453046 (8 Oct 2026)
R7 IF no stocks meet the criteria → THEN cash, whatever the index does. "I'm never really bullish or bearish; only by default. I simply buy stocks that meet my criteria and sell them when they don't work (which is often)." / "If there are no stocks that meet my criteria, I stay in cash." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1844026365508485578 (9 Oct 2024)
R8 IF the indexes take off but few stocks are buyable → THEN wait. "Don't let the indexes pressure into breaking discipline. Until stocks offer buy points based on your own criteria... YOU WAIT!" [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1721543693084049825 (6 Nov 2023)
R9 IF a new up-leg has started after an FTD → THEN a 3–5% index pullback is normal; judge it by how stocks act. "A pullback of 3-5% would be normal during a new up leg after a follow through day." [MM-own words] §7.3
R10 IF the regime is CONFIRMED (FTD in place, distribution ≤ 4 in 25 sessions on the S&P 500 and Nasdaq, breakouts working) → THEN exposure ceiling 100% and scale up per Z5; UNDER PRESSURE (FTD but 5–6 distribution days, or narrow breadth) → ceiling 50%, pilots and half positions only; CORRECTION (no FTD, ≥ 7 distribution days, or both SPY and QQQ fail the index Trend Template) → ceiling 25%, pilots on A+ setups only, otherwise cash. [MM-implied] (desk thresholds; he gives the inputs, not the cut-offs) §7.1–7.7; desk feeds/market/*.md + feeds/setups/latest.json market_pulse
R11 IF the bull market is late stage → THEN switch from investor to trader. "In the beginning of a new bull market you should "invest" buying and holding the strongest stocks for large moves. In the later stages, you should switch to "trader" nailing down decent profits." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1019191018082750464 (17 Jul 2018)
R12 IF leadership narrows (a few mega-caps carry the index, money rotates into laggards) → THEN warning; tighten. "This kind of narrow leadership is more characteristic of a difficult, volatile, potentially late-stage environment than a healthy, broadly advancing market." [MM-own words] §7.6; https://x.com/markminervini/status/2102810522273886230 (23 Sep 2026)
R13 IF breakouts keep meeting selling on days 1–3 → THEN late-stage tape; sell into strength, buy less. "Late stage market action where retail money chases breakouts while institutions sell into strength." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1433068587237904386 (1 Sep 2021)
R14 IF setups appear AND breakouts work AND an FTD prints → THEN get bigger. "Show me stock setups and my toe is in the water. Show me stock breakouts working and a follow through day and I'm in big." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1529887123016495108 (26 May 2022)

⚠️ OVERTRADING: R5, R7, R8 — when the tape is doubtful his answer is cash and patience, not looser criteria.

2. Universe (Trend Template, RS, leaders)

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
U1 IF a stock fails any of the 8 Trend Template criteria → THEN it is not a candidate. (1) price > 150-day and 200-day; (2) 150-day > 200-day; (3) 200-day "trending up for at least 1 month (preferably 4–5 months minimum in most cases)"; (4) 50-day > 150-day and 200-day; (5) price > 50-day; (6) "at least 30 percent above its 52-week low"; (7) "within at least 25 percent of its 52-week high (the closer to a new high the better)"; (8) RS rank "no less than 70". [MM-own words] §1.1–1.8
U2 IF using the desk RS percentile (rs_rank.py) instead of IBD's → THEN keep the 70 cut; prefer ≥ 80 for new buys (the desk SEPA scanner already uses 80). [MM-implied] §1 desk note; setup_sepa.py RS_MIN = 80
U3 IF ranking candidates → THEN prefer leaders: names that make the 52-week-high list first as the market turns up. [MM-own words] §2.5
U4 IF a stock is in a long-term downtrend → THEN don't consider it. "If a stock’s price is in a long-term downtrend, don’t even consider buying it." [MM-own words] §1.9
U5 IF a name is on the 52-week-low list → THEN skip it. "As a general rule, I buy strength not weakness." [MM-own words] §2.4
U6 IF a stock is near a new high → THEN that is a plus, not a reason to wait for a dip: "A stock hitting a new high has no overhead supply to contend with." [MM-own words] §1.11
U7 IF the stock hasn't rallied at least 25–30% off its 52-week low before the base → THEN not yet stage 2. [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR] range, consistent with U1 item 6) §1.10
U8 IF fundamentals are strong but the chart isn't price-ready → THEN no. "A good company is not always a good stock." [MM-own words] §2.2
U9 IF the chart is strong and fundamentals are weak but context explains it (e.g. biotech) → THEN it can qualify; fundamentals tell you the type of trade, price has the final say. [MM-own words] (interview, paraphrase) §2.3
U10 IF a position would be more than ~10% of the stock's average daily volume → THEN it is too big for that name. "Buying up to 10% of the average daily volume is a good max. Rule of thumb." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/2089030125618635021 (16 Aug 2026); https://x.com/markminervini/status/1371912621998415874 (16 Mar 2021)

3. Does the setup qualify? (SEPA, VCP, bases)

3A. VCP and general base rules

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
V1 IF the base shows no contraction of volatility from left to right → THEN it is not constructive. "I’m looking for volatility to contract from left to right." [MM-own words] §3.1–3.2
V2 IF counting contractions (Ts) → THEN 2–6 qualify; 2–4 is typical. "a succession of anywhere from two to six contractions" / "Typically, most VCP setups will be formed by two to four contractions". [MM-own words] §3.3
V3 IF a contraction is much more than about half of the one before it → THEN the pattern is loose; downgrade. Each should be "contained to about half (plus or minus a reasonable amount) of the previous pullback or contraction." Desk check: last T ≤ 0.6 × first T and no T > 1.25 × the one before it (5% zigzag noise allowance). [MM-own words] (quote) / [MM-implied] (tolerances) §3.4
V4 IF the final contraction is wider than 10% → THEN the pivot isn't tight yet; wait. [MM-implied] (his 25 → 15 → 8 example and the 3C pause of 5–10%) §3.3, §4.4
V5 IF volume on the final contraction is not below the 50-day average (with one or two very low days) → THEN no volume dry-up; wait. [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR]) §3.6
V6 IF the base is shorter than 3 weeks → THEN it is not a base (except the power play's 3–6 weeks after a 100% run, V13). "A breakout needs to come out of a base of at least 3-4 weeks min." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/409001087815798784 (6 Dec 2013); §3.8
V7 IF the VCP is treated as its own pattern → THEN correct that: it is a characteristic inside cups, flat bases, power plays and cheats. "it's a characteristic of the patterns." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1285646150733836293 (21 Jul 2020)
V8 IF the base correction is deeper than 50% (or > 60% from the high) → THEN pass: "A correction of more than 50% is generally too much". Normal: 25–35%. [MM-own words] §3.7
V9 IF a stock falls 2–3× more than the market during the correction → THEN avoid it. [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR]) §3.7
V10 IF it is the 4th or 5th base since stage 2 began → THEN late stage; pass or trade small. Bases 1–2 best, 3 "usually still tradable". [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR]) §2.6
V11 IF the base shows a shakeout (undercut of an obvious low that recovers) → THEN that improves the odds. [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR]) §3.8
V12 IF several names are setting up → THEN judge the integrity of each base and its volatility contraction; watch for "Pivot Leakage". [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1645433425967427584 (10 Apr 2023)

3B. Named variants

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
V13 IF power play: +100% or more in under 8 weeks on huge volume, then 3–6 weeks sideways correcting ≤ 20–25% → THEN qualifies without fundamentals; the first surge is "a breakaway move, not an exhaustion move." [MM-implied] (criteria via [SEC-BR]) / [MM-own words] (quote) §9.1–9.5
V14 IF flat base: 4–7 weeks, about 10–15% high to low → THEN qualifies without a full VCP count. [MM-own words] §3.5
V15 IF Cup Completion Cheat (3C): stock already up 25–100%+ over 3–36 months, cup 3–45 weeks, pause 5–10% deep on light volume → THEN the pause high is an early pivot. He says cheats are still breakouts: "I never deviated from buying breakouts by buying at what I call a "cheat" or "low cheat."" [MM-implied] (criteria via [SEC-BR]) / [MM-own words] (quote) §4.4
V16 IF IPO primary base → THEN at least 3–5 weeks and no more than a 25–35% correction (≤ 25% for a 3-week base). [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR]) §4.7
V17 IF a breakout failed as the market turned down, then the stock rebuilt tighter and re-emerges → THEN valid ("Failure Reset"). [MM-own words] §3.9

4. Entry

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
N1 IF price is not at a defined pivot → THEN no buy. "I have a strict discipline that only allows me to buy at a very specific point: the point at which reward outweighs risk." [MM-own words] §4.1
N2 IF locating the pivot → THEN it is "The high of the pivot within the "handle" or "cheat" or "low cheat" area". [MM-own words] §4.3
N3 IF price clears the pivot → THEN buy only if projected full-day volume beats the 50-day average; desk bar: ≥ 1.4× the 50-day average on the breakout day. Don't front-run a tight pivot. [MM-implied] (MM: above-average volume, via [SEC-BR]; 1.4× is Chris's desk threshold, inside his range; the desk scanners use 1.5×, see C9) §4.2; desk watchlist.md
N4 IF the breakout bar closes in the lower half of its range → THEN no buy that day (a "squat" gets 1–2 days to recover, M2). [MM-implied] §4.8; setup_sepa.py B3_upper_half_close
N5 IF price is more than 5% past the pivot → THEN too extended; pass (ideal fill ≤ 2% over the pivot). He: "too extended to buy here if you use tight stops... could easily pull back 10 or 15%." [MM-own words] (quote) / [MM-implied] (5% / 2% numbers; desk MAX_EXT = 5) https://x.com/markminervini/status/930537420176707586 (14 Nov 2017)
N6 IF the low-cheat entry sits below the 50-day → THEN allowed only if ≤ 8% below it and Trend Template items 1–4 still hold. "Low cheat entries can be below the 50 day, but not more than 8% below is my general guideline." [MM-own words] §4.5, §0.2
N7 IF you are about to buy → THEN have the plan written: "1. Initial stop-loss 2. Re-entry criteria 3. Criteria for nailing down profits into strength 4. When to sell into weakness to protect your profit 5. How you will handle catastrophic situations" [MM-own words] §4.9
N8 IF the stock gaps more than 3% over the pivot at the open → THEN no chase; wait for a new pivot. [MM-implied] (desk MAX_GAP = 3) setup_sepa.py B5_gap_open_3pct
N9 IF the stop distance from the fill would exceed 8% → THEN pass, or wait for a tighter cheat entry. [MM-own words] ("Max stop 8%") https://x.com/markminervini/status/1220334088097353728 (23 Jan 2020); §0.2
N10 IF it's a power play or low-cheat bottom-fishing pivot → THEN aggressive; only in a stock you really want to own. "Would only do this on a stock I really like fundamentally and want to own." [MM-own words] §4.6

5. Stop

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
S1 IF entering → THEN the stop is set before the buy and placed. "Define your stop before you enter a trade" (mug). "I always have a stop. An initial stop loss." [MM-own words] §5.12, §6.4
S2 IF sizing the stop → THEN no more than half your real average gain: "the amount of loss should be no more than one-half the amount of expected gain based on one’s real-life trading results." (15% average gain → stop ≤ 7.5%.) [MM-own words] §5.3
S3 IF the stop would be wider than 10% → THEN never. "not allow any stock to fall more than 10 percent before selling." Desk normal max 8%: "Max stop 8%". Typical: "Stops are generally between 4 and 8%." [MM-own words] §5.4; https://x.com/markminervini/status/1220334088097353728; https://x.com/markminervini/status/1319388911089627137 (22 Oct 2020)
S4 IF the stop is hit → THEN sell at once at the next bid. "get out immediately! Take whatever the next bid price is." [MM-own words] §5.6
S5 IF you are holding a loss hoping it comes back → THEN it's already a loss. "A loss is a loss once it becomes a loss, not after you sell it. If you are not managing risk in real-time, you are not managing risk." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/2085483309467914714 (6 Aug 2026)
S6 IF the market is tough → THEN tighten stops (7–8% → 5–6%). [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR]) §5.8
S7 IF the market gets frothy → THEN stops tighter, profits sooner, protect breakeven sooner, and use a time stop: "I sometimes even sell stocks just because they don't move on cue; a time stop." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1352411615392620547 (22 Jan 2021)
S8 IF you think stops cost you money → THEN remember why: "Why I do use stops? Because I rely on probabilities, not a crystal ball." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1311411575614574594 (30 Sep 2020)

6. Size and progressive exposure

ID Rule (IF → THEN) Tag Source
Z1 IF sizing a trade → THEN risk no more than 1.25–2.5% of equity: "your maximum risk should be no more than 1.25 to 2.5 percent of your equity on any one trade." Desk normal 1.25%, cap 2.5%. [MM-own words] / desk default [MM-implied] §5.1, §0.2
Z2 IF computing size → THEN position % = risk % ÷ stop %. "if you have a 10% stop, your position size can't be more than 12.5%. Or a 25% position can't have a stop greater than 5%." / "25% position size x 5% stop = 1.25% of total equity." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/988883629530058752 (24 Apr 2018); https://x.com/markminervini/status/1673035054434402304 (25 Jun 2023)
Z3 IF you can't make money with an 8–10% max stop and 1.25–2.5% risk → THEN the problem is selection and timing, not sizing. "If you can't be successful trading stock using a max stop of 8-10% and position sizing for 1.25% to 2.5% of total equity, then you have a much bigger problem with your selection and timing criteria." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/988952437154308097 (25 Apr 2018)
Z4 IF coming out of cash → THEN start with pilot buys; "I rarely jump right in with both feet." [MM-own words] §7.4; https://x.com/markminervini/status/1720487045469028372 (3 Nov 2023)
Z5 IF you are not profitable at your current exposure → THEN don't increase it. "If you’re not profitable at 25% or 50% invested, why move up to 75% or 100% invested or use margin? Wait for confirmation and require that at least a few trades work out before getting more aggressive. This is what I call Progressive Exposure". Desk steps: 25% → 50% → 75% → 100%, one step only after ≥ 2 of the last 3 new trades are ≥ +1R. [MM-own words] (quote) / [MM-implied] (step test) https://x.com/markminervini/status/1265399599067475972 (26 May 2020); https://x.com/markminervini/status/1884705597402059074 (29 Jan 2025)
Z6 IF you hit an abnormal losing streak → THEN scale down (his example: 5,000 → 2,000 → 1,000-share lots) and step back up only when the plan works again. "Never get bold when running cold". Desk trigger: 2 consecutive losing closed trades → halve the next position's risk; 4 → pilot size only. [MM-own words] (quote, lot example) / [MM-implied] (2/4 trigger) §5.7, §5.12; charts.py losing_streak()
Z7 IF trades are working → THEN get aggressive, but never risk more than 1–2% per trade. "When I do get aggressive, I get very aggressive, but always with a stop loss and never risking more than 1-2% of total equity per trade." [MM-own words] https://x.com/markminervini/status/1844026365508485578 (9 Oct 2024)
Z8 IF counting positions → THEN concentrate: 4–8 names (desk); never > 20. [MM-implied] ([SEC-BR] 4–6 / 10–12; §0.2 desk default) §5.9, §0.2
Z9 IF the market turns risky → THEN the risk-off list: off margin, raise 25–50% cash, smaller sizes, nail down profits, tighter stops, sell what breaks down. [MM-own words] (abridged list) §5.8

15. Notes on method

  • X pull (@markminervini, user id 105353526). Same access method as the Qullamaggie desk: the user's X connector (x.search_posts_all, read-only, official API). Keyword-themed queries (the desk's X-post archive: stop, risk, size, sell, market, VCP, Trend Template, setups, earnings, entry, mindset, style) across time windows from 2009 to 11 Oct 2026; 25 posts per call. 2,898 unique posts retrieved (his account has ~38,600 posts; the method returns keyword matches only, so this is the rule-dense slice, not the full history). 155 long posts were re-fetched with get_posts_by_ids to get the full note_tweet text.

  • Quotes. Every quoted X line was copied from the fetched text (full note_tweet where truncated). NOTES quotes are reused as NOTES cites them.
  • Not used: lookalike accounts (@markmineveini, @markminervinipl); paywalled Minervini Private Access / Markets 360; books (not read directly).
  • Desk thresholds (R10 cut-offs, 1.4× volume, 5%/2% chase limits, 2/4 losing-streak triggers, climax thresholds, 10-session time stop) are [MM-implied] choices inside his stated ranges; change them freely.
