1. Business model and revenue mix

The world's largest outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) provider (ATM = assembly, test, materials). It also runs EMS (electronic manufacturing services) through its USI subsidiary. The AI angle is leading-edge advanced packaging ("LEAP": CoWoS-type, flip-chip, SiP) plus test.

6. Competitive position / moat

  • It is the #1 OSAT by scale, with the Taiwan cluster next to TSMC and a "pure-play" position (it doesn't compete with its customers).
  • It has first-mover advantages in advanced packaging and test capacity, a scarce resource in AI hardware. The company says it is stepping up capacity, automation and R&D.
  • Weaknesses: the EMS arm is low-margin (2–3% operating margin) and dilutes the mix. TSMC does its own advanced packaging (CoWoS) and competes in the high end.

7. Bull / Bear

Bull

  • Growth is accelerating at every level: quarterly revenue +27% and monthly revenue +46% in August. EPS +171%.
  • Margins are expanding for a fifth straight quarter (ATM gross margin guided to 28–29% for Q3).
  • FY guide raised: ATM +35%, general segment +20% (from 13%), LEAP ahead of US$3.5B.
  • AI packaging and test capacity is a structural bottleneck. Testing is growing faster (+42%).

Bear

  • Very capex-heavy (about US$4.1B in 1H26). Net leverage is rising and FCF is thin.
  • It is cyclical: OSAT margins historically mean-revert. The stock is up about 287% in a year (per the daily report).
  • Valuation is stretched versus Amkor. Price is about at the average target.
  • Taiwan/China geopolitics and US trade and tariff policy. NT$ strength is an FX headwind.
  • The Q3 growth jump leans heavily on low-margin EMS (+40% QoQ).

What would change the view: Monthly revenue growth dropping back below +25% YoY. Q3 gross margin below 20.5%. A capex cut or customer pushouts after the Micron (30 Sep) or TSMC (15 Oct) prints. Trade or tariff escalation.

  • Dilution: low to moderate. Shares outstanding are 4.47B, including treasury (6-K). stockanalysis shows +1.6% YoY. The diluted weighted count (4.506B) is about 2.7% above basic (4.387B), driven by employee option plans (2018/2023) and convertible bonds issued by subsidiaries (20-F). No parent-level convertible was found [not exhaustively verified]. Bonds payable rose from NT$2.0B to NT$15.0B in Q2; the terms were not checked.
  • Insider selling: n/a. ASX is a foreign private issuer, so no Form 4s are filed. Taiwan insider filings were not checked [unverified].
  • Concentration: ATM top 10 customers = 60%, with one customer >10%. Heavy AI/computing exposure ties it to a few hyperscaler/GPU supply chains.
  • Other: geopolitical (Taiwan), FX (guide assumes 31.9 NT$/US$), rising interest expense (NT$1.85B net in Q2), and a supply chain gated by Micron and TSMC.

9. Fundamental grade (SEPA lens)

Strong. Sales and EPS are accelerating (revenue +27% in Q2, +46% in August; EPS +171%), margins have expanded for five quarters, and guidance was raised, which supports the cup-with-handle. The caveats are low absolute margins, capex-driven leverage and cyclicality. Research only, not advice.
