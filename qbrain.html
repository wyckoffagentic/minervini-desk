Q BRAIN — a long-only what would Qullamaggie do decision rulebook

Research only, not advice. Built 11 Oct 2026 (Sydney) for Chris's desk.

What this is. A step-by-step, long-only rulebook that tries to take discretion out of live trading. It encodes Kristjan Kullamägi's (KK, "Qullamaggie") overall approach: regime first, a narrow universe of liquid high-range leaders, two setups (breakout/flag and episodic pivot), a low-of-day stop capped by ADR, small risk per trade, and mechanical selling. It is deliberately not a recipe for trading often. KK calls overtrading his biggest weakness, so the brain biases toward fewer, better trades and flags each place where he warns against overtrading (look for ⚠️ OVERTRADING).

What this is not. No short selling of any kind. KK's parabolic-short setup, his short-side stops and covers, and every short-related post are left out on purpose. Where a KK rule was stated for shorts only, it is not used here.

Backbone and additions. The backbone is the desk's Qullamaggie notes (blog, streams, interviews, book write-ups), cited as §n. Additions come from KK's and James Muninn's X posts, pulled 11 Oct 2026 into the desk's X-post archive and cited by URL.

0.1 Daily operating sequence (pre-commitment)

  1. Night before: run regime (§1) and scans (§2); build a watchlist of ≤ ~50 names; write the trigger, stop and share count for every candidate before the open (N9).
  2. Open (first 30–60 minutes): execute only pre-written triggers. No new names that were not on the list unless they are an EP that passes E1–E11.
  3. After the open: manage by rule only (D, P, T). Set hard stops (S7).
  4. Evening: log every trade with the review rubric (NOTES §11.4). Count new trades for the day (O3).

3. Does the setup qualify?

KK trades three setups; this long-only brain uses two: the breakout/flag and the episodic pivot (EP). "you only need ONE solid setup to make 10s of millions" (https://x.com/Qullamaggie/status/1331870672440745984).
