How this page works

RECENT (default) = triggered within the last 10 completed daily sessions with the stop not hit since (the trigger-day close and every later daily low above the stop), plus pending plans whose last close is still above the stop and within 10% of the buy stop. SHOW ALL = every open / pending plan; rows that fail the RECENT test carry a ⏸ reason (e.g. "triggered 20 sessions ago", "stop hit 09-21", "price already under the stop"); EMA10 flips have no stop, so they only show under SHOW ALL. Your choice is remembered on this device. A ticker's main plan prefers one that passes the test.

Source: trade_pct.plan_index() - the same plan + P/L block every page and chart uses. A ticker with any open (triggered) plan is listed under Triggered with its main plan (Setup Master > SEPA > VCP > power play > HTF > flat base > TR spring > uptrend spring > Wyckoff ticket > MA Stack GUD > Spring→Bull > EMA10 flip); its other plans are under "other plans". % P/L = (last completed close − entry) ÷ entry; R = that move ÷ (entry − stop); days = sessions since the trigger. Pending = distance from the last close to the buy stop. $ figures = a position of the size above bought at the buy stop: shares = floor(size ÷ entry); $ risk = shares × (entry − stop); 2R / 3R = entry ± 2 / 3 × (entry − stop). Families: Minervini = SEPA / VCP / power play / HTF / flat base (+ Setup Master picks from them); Wyckoff = springs + Structure tickets; EMA = EMA10 flips + the EMA-pullback watch; MA Stack = GUD + Spring→Bull. Liquidity floor: ADV50 ≥ $20M · price ≥ $5 on the RS feed; names without RS data are hidden too.
