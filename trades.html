SHOW ALL = every open / pending plan; rows that fail the RECENT test carry a ⏸ reason (e.g. "triggered 20 sessions ago", "stop hit 09-21", "price already under the stop"); EMA10 flips have no stop, so they only show under SHOW ALL. Source: trade_pct.plan_index() - the same plan + P/L block every page and chart uses.
